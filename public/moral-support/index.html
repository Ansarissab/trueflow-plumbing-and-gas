What is Moral Support?

Every month, the Moral Lads pick a home with a problem that's making life harder than it needs to be, and fix it for free. We film the job, share the story your way, and show how it's done.

  • We fix what matters

    No hot water, blocked sewer, dodgy wiring, leaks. The essentials that make a home safe and liveable.

  • Free. No catch.

    Labour and materials covered, done by licensed plumbers and electricians. Funded by Tradie AI.

  • Your story, your way

    On camera, face blurred, or just the house and the work. You see the video before anyone else does.

How it works

  1. Apply

    Fill in the form below. Photos of the problem help a lot.

  2. We read every one

    Our team reviews applications, calls the people we shortlist, and visits to check the job.

  3. We fix it

    The Lads come out and get it done properly, with mates from other trades when the job needs it.

  4. We share the story

    The episode drops on the first Monday of the month on Moral Lads, the way you're comfortable with.

Who it's for

People doing it tough who can't get essential work done on the home they live in. That might be money, illness, getting older, disability, caring for someone, or doing it all alone.

What we can help with

  • No hot water, blocked sewer or toilet, burst or leaking pipes
  • Unsafe or faulty electrics, no power, dead smoke alarms
  • Accessibility: grab rails, ramps, handheld showers
  • Essential maintenance that affects health or safety

What we can't help with

  • Cosmetic renovations or upgrades
  • Investment properties
  • Work already covered by insurance or a warranty
  • Homes outside the Gold Coast and surrounds (for now)

    Questions

    Does it really cost nothing?

    Yes. Labour and materials are covered. There's no catch and nothing to sign up to.

    Who picks the jobs?

    Our team reads every application, shortlists, and selects the jobs where we can make the biggest difference.

    Do I have to be on camera?

    No. We can blur your face or just film the home and the work. You'll see the video before it goes live.

    Where do you work?

    The Gold Coast, from Coolangatta to Yatala, for now. We'll grow the area as the series grows.

    What happens after I apply?

    If your application sends successfully, you'll land on a thank-you page. Our team reads every application, and if we can help, we'll get back to you by email.
