Our story

From one ute to two trades.

2019
How it started

Moral Service started back in 2019 as a one-man plumbing gig under the name Moral Plumbing, founded by Shaun. Fresh out of his apprenticeship and newly licensed as a contractor, Shaun took the leap as a one-man band: nothing but his ute, his tools, and a strong, honest work ethic. He began sharing the plumbing on social media, from job sites to small wins, and local support quickly followed.

Today
Where we’re at

As the work grew, Shaun’s brother Justin, a licensed electrician, joined him, and together they formed Moral Service: plumbing and electrical under one family name, built on trust, quality, and long-term value for Gold Coast locals. Homeowners find them online and book because the work gets done right.

Owners

Shaun · Owner & Plumber

Every Moral plumbing job is Shaun's. He takes real pride in plumbing done properly; it's never just fixing the issue, the finished job has to look right too. He won't leave until he can stand back and say, "That's done right."

The electrical side is Justin's alone, and more than ten years in the game shows in the small things: a clean worksite, safety put first, and a finish he's proud to hand over.
