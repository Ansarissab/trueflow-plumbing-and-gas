Problems

The faults we know by heart.

Power keeps tripping

A safety switch that trips is doing its job; the question is why. We find the circuit or appliance behind it and fix the cause.

Flickering lights or tired switches

Loose connections and worn switches are quick to sort now, and expensive to ignore.

Aircon won't start, or trips the board

We test the unit and its circuit, then repair or re-wire whichever one is at fault.

Never enough power points

If every wall has a powerboard, we add proper outlets where you actually use them.

One part of the house has gone dark

A dead circuit usually traces back to one fault. We track it down and restore power safely.

FAQ

Asked all the time.

Do you install smoke alarms?

We do, and we issue the certification the same day the alarms go in, which matters for rentals.

How do I know my switchboard needs upgrading?

A few signs give it away: ceramic fuses, no safety switches, or breakers that trip whenever the house is busy. If any of those sound familiar, it's worth an inspection.

Do you supply and install air conditioning?

Yes. We supply, install and service split-system units, and handle the electrical circuit they run on as part of the same job.

Can you do the electrical for a renovation?

That's regular work for us, from single-room renos to full new builds, wired to plan and certified at the end.

My hot water system is electric. Do I call a plumber or an electrician?

Either way, you'd call us. Justin handles the hot water wiring and upgrades, and Shaun covers the plumbing half of the same job.
